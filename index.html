What is this???
This is a little tool Jake and I made to explore the connectivity of oceans basins along iso-surfaces of z.

Basically, if you click two places on the map (left) it will tell you the deepest depth those two points are connected. The map on the right shows the connected level set the two points share. I reccomend starting with two points near each other.

Its very much experimental, and made completely in javascript which has really bad support for matrix mathy sciency stuff so pardon the slowness

If you use it shoot me an email at gdf at ucla.edu because I'd get a kick out of that.
